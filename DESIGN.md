# TaxPulse Design System (`DESIGN.md`)

This design system governs the editorial redesign of **TaxPulse — Advocates & Tax Litigation Experts** (`https://www.taxpulse.com/`). It synthesizes the domain-matched design system generated by **UI/UX Pro Max** (`tooling/ui-ux-pro-max-skill`, commit `2de1134dc4d1cfe112934611ae5803ae17a1bd7e`, output saved in `tooling/design-system/taxpulse/`) with the editorial layout, typographic hierarchy, and quality gates of **Hallmark** (`tooling/hallmark`, commit `04977e15f3dfd6ee4b74c887dac3eb52665d6c92`).

---

## 1. Visual Direction & Editorial Posture

- **Genre & Tone**: Restrained Legal Editorial (`editorial` / `vellum` craft rules). Built for a Pakistani tax litigation, constitutional advocacy, and corporate advisory law firm established in 2003 (Sindh Bar Council Registration No. 351).
- **Content-First Mandate**: Every headline, testimonial, case reference, bio, office address, CTA label, and legal article preserves verbatim TaxPulse source wording. Design provides structural clarity, generous reading measure, scannable hierarchy, and accessible controls without invented copy or decorative bloat.
- **UI/UX Pro Max Pattern**: *Trust & Authority + Conversion* — Clear primary consultation pathway paired with verifiable proof (client roster, verbatim client testimonials, reported High Court / Tribunal judgments, and national office presence across Karachi, Lahore, Islamabad, and Multan).

## 3. Typography & Reading Scale

- **Display / Editorial Serif**: `'EB Garamond', Georgia, serif` (`--font-display`) — used for `h1`–`h3`, pull quotes, case titles, and article titles. `font-synthesis: none` ensures authentic italic/roman weights without synthetic slanting.
- **Body / Interface Sans**: `'Lato', system-ui, -apple-system, sans-serif` (`--font-body`) — used for body prose, navigation, statutory citations, metadata, tables, and form controls.
- **Type Scale & Measure**:
  - Hero `h1`: `clamp(2.35rem, 4.2vw, 3.75rem)`, line-height `1.08`, letter-spacing `-0.02em`
  - Page `h1`: `clamp(2rem, 3.2vw, 3rem)`, line-height `1.12`
  - Section `h2`: `clamp(1.65rem, 2.4vw, 2.25rem)`, line-height `1.18`
  - Card / Article `h3`: `1.35rem`–`1.5rem`, line-height `1.28`
  - Body text: `1rem` (`16px` UI) to `1.0625rem` (`17px` long-form articles), line-height `1.65`–`1.75`
  - Micro metadata / Eyebrows: `0.75rem`–`0.8125rem` (`12px`–`13px`), uppercase tracking `0.1em`, tabular numerals where dates/citations appear.
  - **Reading Measure**: Long-form article bodies, biographies, and testimonials are constrained to `max-w-[68ch]` (`65ch`–`70ch`) for comfortable scanning.

## 4. Layout, Spacing & Structural Rules

- **Container**: Max width `1200px` (`75rem`), horizontal padding `1rem` (`16px`) on small mobile (`360px`–`639px`), `1.5rem` (`24px`) on tablet (`768px`), `2rem` (`32px`) on desktop (`1024px+`).
- **Spacing Rhythm**: Uses UI/UX Pro Max tokens (`4px`, `8px`, `16px`, `24px`, `32px`, `48px`, `64px`) with section vertical padding `3.5rem` (mobile) to `5rem` (desktop).
- **Editorial Dividers (Hallmark Rules)**:
  - Use clean `1px solid var(--color-border)` horizontal and vertical rules to organize legal modules, office directories, and reported cases.
  - **Prohibited**: Thick `border-l-4` decorative callout bars, static rounded pill badges for categories/tags/statuses, middle-dot-joined multi-field strings inside cards, and `100vh` height locks (use `min-h-full` or proportional spacing instead).
  - **Overflow Discipline**: `html, body { overflow-x: clip; }` so sticky navigation works properly and `360px` viewports never suffer horizontal scrollbars.

   - Dedicated accessible mobile drawer (`aria-expanded`, `Escape` close, expandable "Our Firm" sub-links, `44px` minimum tap targets).
2. **Hero & Consultation Priority (Hallmark Redesign)**:
   - Left column: Original headline ("Pakistan’s Leading Tax Consultancy"), verbatim Zaheer Allana quote, and immediate action row (`Ask for Free Tax Advice!` / `Get Free Advice!` primary CTA + `Our Team` / `Get in Touch`).
   - Right panel: Dedicated consultation action card featuring the verbatim TaxPulse offer ("Get Free Tax Advice" — "TaxPulse offers you FREE consultation with our principal advocate...") and direct links to the four regional offices and consultation form.
3. **Cards, Service Grids & Tabs**:
   - Crisp `1px` bordered white surfaces (`border-radius: 6px`–`8px` editorial precision—never bubbly `24px` consumer-app corners) with top hairline accents on hover (`transition: border-color 180ms ease, box-shadow 180ms ease, transform 180ms ease`; never `transition: all`).
   - Practice Areas uses clean scannable columns + expandable/anchor sections for Litigation Services, Tax Compliance Services, Advisory Services, and Retainer-ship Services without omitting a single list item.
4. **Articles Hub & Reader**:
   - Filter by genuine category (`All`, `Income Tax`, `International Taxation`, `Sales Tax`), keyword search, archive month/year filter, and pagination (`/articles/`, `/articles/page/2/`, etc.) matching live WP structure while keeping out-of-scope compromised posts excluded.
   - Individual article view preserves full original prose, statutory headings, tables, embedded YouTube videos, downloadable PDFs (`International_Tax_Handbook-2.pdf`, FBR Ordinance link), and clean citation formatting with zero raw `[vc_...]` shortcodes.
5. **Gallery & Accessible Lightbox**:
   - Album overview (`/gallery/`) + dedicated routes for all 8 live albums (`/opening-ceremony-of-tax-pulse-in-multan/`, `/icma-event-2/`, `/annual-dinner/`, `/summer-camp-2023/`, `/annual-dinner-2023/`, `/seminar-on-income-tax/`, `/lecture-fbr-multan-2025/`, `/lahore-office-of-taxpulse/`).
   - Keyboard-accessible lightbox (`Escape` closes, `ArrowLeft`/`ArrowRight` navigates, visible focus ring, focus trapped while open, focus strictly returned to the triggering thumbnail button on close).
6. **Consultation Form & Office Directory**:
   - Preserves the exact live TaxPulse Contact Form 7 fields (`your-name`, `your-company`, `your-email`, `tel`, `your-message`, `"Send"` button) with explicit accessible `<label>` elements, inline validation errors (`aria-invalid`, `role="alert"`), and a transparent non-production preview confirmation panel (no data transmitted to `taxpulse.com`).
   - Displays all four offices (Karachi, Lahore, Islamabad, Multan) with branch heads, exact verbatim addresses, clickable `tel:` numbers, `mailto:info@taxpulse.com`, Google Maps links, and the embedded Lahore Badar Centre map on `/contact-us/`.

## 6. Accessibility & Motion Rules

- **Contrast**: Minimum `4.5:1` for normal text, `3:1` for large headings and UI borders.
- **Keyboard & Focus**: Every link, button, dropdown trigger, accordion, tab, and lightbox control is keyboard-operable with `outline: 3px solid var(--color-ring); outline-offset: 2px` on `:focus-visible`.
- **Reduced Motion**: Wrap transitions and Framer Motion / CSS entrances with `@media (prefers-reduced-motion: reduce)` and `MotionConfig reducedMotion="user"`.
- **Target Breakpoints**: Audited at `360px`, `390px`, `768px`, `1024px`, and `1440px` with zero horizontal overflow (`scrollWidth <= innerWidth`) and `44x44px` minimum interactive targets on touch controls.

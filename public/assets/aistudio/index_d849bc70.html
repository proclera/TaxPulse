Before you contact us

Questions worth asking first.

Clear information helps you take the next step without confusion.

I have received an FBR notice. What should I do first?

Keep the full notice, note its date of service and response deadline, and identify the issuing authority. Do not assume that sending an enquiry extends any legal deadline.

Is the first consultation free?

The initial enquiry is for suitability and conflict screening. The scope and any fee for a substantive professional consultation will be confirmed before an appointment. No free legal advice is promised here.

Can I upload confidential documents here?

Not in this demonstration. The production website should use a separate secure channel only after independent testing and approval.

Can TaxPulse advise overseas Pakistanis?

The firm may assess matters involving Pakistan tax residence, income, assets, withholding and treaty questions, subject to the facts and applicable law.
